What fits

One room, a lot of ways to use it.

The tables and benches move, the floor clears, and the lamp stays on. Here's how the room sets up for the gatherings people book it for most. If yours isn't here, ask us.

Startup circle

Founders swapping notes, honest feedback and a whiteboard's worth of ideas. Long tables go into a U so everyone faces everyone, and nobody's stuck at the back.
