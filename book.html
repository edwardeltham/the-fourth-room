Questions

Questions people ask.

Where is The Fourth Room?

214, South First Cross Street, Kapaleeshwarar Nagar, Neelankarai, Chennai 600041. The room is on the ground floor, so there are no stairs to climb. Tap the address on this page to open it in Google Maps.

How many people fit?

Up to 20 people seated at the tables and benches. For movement classes like dance or theatre, around 15 is comfortable.

Is the room air-conditioned?

Yes, the whole room is air-conditioned.

Can we move the furniture?

Yes. The tables and benches can make a U-shape, classroom rows, one long table or a circle, or be pushed back to clear the floor. Tell us the setup when you book and we'll have it ready.

Can I book the podcast studio on its own?

Yes. The studio can be booked by the hour on its own or together with the main room.

What does it cost?

The room is ₹2,499 for 3 hours, and the podcast studio is ₹999 an hour. Message us with your date and plan and we'll confirm the booking.

Can I bring food and drinks?

Message us about what you're planning and we'll let you know what works.
