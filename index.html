Where we are

A small event space in Neelankarai, on ECR.

The Fourth Room is a community space for hire on the ECR side of Neelankarai in south Chennai, close to Palavakkam, Kottivakkam, Injambakkam, Thiruvanmiyur and Akkarai, and an easy drive from Sholinganallur and OMR. People book it as a workshop venue, a training room, a meeting room, a small party hall for birthdays and baby showers, or a place to run a weekly class, all for groups of up to 20.

Creators book the podcast studio by the hour to record podcasts, YouTube videos, Instagram reels and voice-overs, and the main room works for a live podcast recording with an audience of up to 20.
